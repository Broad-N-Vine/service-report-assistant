## Safety Language to Preserve

Use language like:

AI-generated wording is a draft. Review service details, parts, pricing, readings, warranty language, safety wording, customer-specific details, and company rules before use.

For estimate-related pages, also include:

Review recommended work, pricing, options, warranty language, exclusions, and customer approval requirements before sending.

---

## Link Rules

- Use relative `.html` links.
- Do not link to `/api/` endpoints.
- Do not link to `thank-you.html`.
- Do not label live tools as “coming soon.”
- Preserve existing MailerLite blocks.
- Preserve affiliate disclosures before affiliate links.
- Keep review-first language visible near AI tool CTAs.
- Do not add fake readings, prices, warranties, guarantees, or safety claims.
