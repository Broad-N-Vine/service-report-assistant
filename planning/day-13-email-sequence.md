# Day 13 — Email Sequence Setup

## Goal

Create a simple email sequence for HVAC AI Helper subscribers.

The sequence should:
- Help the reader first
- Build trust
- Send people back to useful pages/tools
- Recommend relevant resources carefully
- Prepare future interest in the HVAC Paperwork Starter Kit

---

## Current Email Capture Promise

The site currently tells visitors they can get:
- HVAC/R technician note examples
- Service report templates
- Invoice wording examples
- Follow-up message templates
- Helpful paperwork tips

The emails below should match that promise.

---

## Important Rules

- Keep emails helpful, not pushy.
- Do not claim the tools are fully AI-powered until the backend is connected.
- Do not promise income, savings, better performance, legal compliance, warranty coverage, safety, or fewer repairs.
- Include affiliate disclosure when recommending Jobber or QuickBooks.
- Remind users to review wording before sending it to customers.
- Send people back to pages that match the email topic.
