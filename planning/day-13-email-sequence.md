## Email 2 — Service Report Cleanup

### Timing

1 day after signup.

### Subject

A simple checklist for better HVAC service reports

### Preview Text

Use this before sending service paperwork to a customer.

### Body

Hi there,

One of the easiest ways to improve HVAC service paperwork is to check whether the report clearly answers five questions:

1. What did the customer report?
2. What did the technician find?
3. What work was completed?
4. What should the customer know next?
5. What needs office review before sending?

I put together a simple checklist here:

HVAC Service Report Checklist  
https://service-report-assistant.pages.dev/hvac-service-report-checklist.html

You can use it before sending service reports, invoice descriptions, or customer follow-up messages.

You can also use the main generator here:

HVAC Service Report Generator  
https://service-report-assistant.pages.dev/hvac-service-report-generator.html

Tip: Do not invent readings, parts, pricing, warranty terms, or final system condition unless they are confirmed in the technician notes.

Thanks,  
HVAC AI Helper

## Email 3 — Invoice and Follow-Up Wording

### Timing

3 days after signup.

### Subject

Service report wording vs invoice wording

### Preview Text

They should not always say the same thing.

### Body

Hi there,

A service report, invoice description, and customer follow-up message should not always use the same wording.

A service report can include more detail.

An invoice description should usually be shorter and focused on the confirmed work performed.

A customer follow-up should be friendly, simple, and easy to read.

Here are two useful pages:

HVAC Invoice Wording Examples  
https://service-report-assistant.pages.dev/hvac-invoice-wording-examples.html

HVAC Customer Follow-Up Text Generator  
https://service-report-assistant.pages.dev/hvac-customer-follow-up-text-generator.html

Before sending anything to a customer, review the wording for pricing, warranty language, parts, recommendations, and company-specific details.
