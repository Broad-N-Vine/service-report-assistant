Quick Answer

What should an HVAC repair follow-up text include?

An HVAC repair follow-up text should thank the customer, briefly summarize the confirmed repair, mention any supported next step, and invite the customer to contact the company if they notice another issue.

The text should stay short and factual. Do not invent parts, pricing, warranty terms, system condition, safety claims, maintenance-plan details, or performance guarantees unless those details are confirmed in the technician notes.

How to use it

Write a better HVAC repair follow-up text in three steps

1. Start with the repair notes

Use the technician’s real notes, including what the customer reported, what was found, what was repaired, and what should be reviewed.

2. Keep it brief

A text message should usually be shorter than a full service report or email. Focus on what the customer needs to know next.

3. Review before sending

Confirm repair details, recommendations, pricing, warranty language, scheduling links, and customer-specific notes before sending.

Technician Notes

Example rough technician notes

Customer reported AC not cooling. Found failed capacitor. Replaced capacitor with approved part. System started after repair and general cooling operation was checked. Recommended customer monitor system and continue routine maintenance.

These notes include the customer issue, technician finding, completed repair, post-repair check, and recommendation.

Examples

HVAC repair follow-up text message examples

Use these examples as starting points. Adjust the wording to match the actual technician notes, your company voice, and the customer situation.

Simple repair follow-up text

Thanks for having us out today. We completed the HVAC repair noted during the visit and checked general system operation afterward. Please contact us if you notice any additional comfort or system concerns.

AC no-cooling repair text

Thank you for today’s service visit. We diagnosed the AC no-cooling issue, completed the approved repair, and checked general operation after service. Please monitor the system and let us know if you notice any additional cooling concerns.

Capacitor replacement follow-up text

Thanks for choosing us today. We replaced the confirmed failed capacitor and checked general AC operation after the repair. Please contact us if the system does not continue operating as expected.

Furnace repair follow-up text

Thank you for having us out today. We checked the furnace for the reported heating issue, completed the documented service work, and checked operation afterward. Please let us know if you notice any additional heating concerns.

Drain cleaning follow-up text

Thanks for today’s service visit. We cleared the documented condensate drain issue and checked drainage after service. Please monitor the area and contact us if you notice any additional water or drainage concerns.

Maintenance recommendation text

Thank you for today’s HVAC service visit. The repair notes have been documented, and we recommend continuing routine maintenance to help keep the system on schedule. Please contact our office if you would like help scheduling the next visit.
Review before sending: Confirm the completed repair, customer name, part details, recommendations, pricing, warranty language, scheduling details, and company-specific wording before sending any text message.
Before and After

Vague vs. better repair follow-up text

Too vague

Fixed it. Thanks.

This is short, but it does not give the customer enough context or a helpful next step.

Better

Thanks for today’s service visit. We completed the approved repair and checked general system operation afterward. Please contact us if you notice any additional comfort or system concerns.

This is still brief, but it sounds more professional and gives the customer a clear next step.

Invoice Wording

HVAC repair invoice wording example

Completed approved HVAC repair and checked general system operation after service.

Invoice wording should usually be shorter than the customer follow-up message. Confirm pricing, parts, labor, taxes, discounts, warranty language, and invoice totals before sending.

Common Mistakes

Common HVAC repair follow-up text mistakes to avoid

  • Being too vague: avoid texts like “fixed it” without a helpful customer summary.
  • Inventing details: do not add parts, readings, pricing, or system condition unless documented.
  • Overpromising: do not guarantee performance, safety, lower bills, or no future problems.
  • Adding unsupported warranty terms: only include warranty language your company has approved.
  • Skipping review: customer names, scheduling links, recommendations, and repair details should be checked before sending.

FAQ

FAQ

What should I text a customer after an HVAC repair?

Thank the customer, briefly summarize the completed repair, mention any supported next step, and invite the customer to contact the company if they notice another issue.

Should the text include pricing?

Only include pricing if it has been reviewed and approved by your office. Many follow-up texts should simply summarize the visit and point the customer to the invoice.

Should the text mention warranty?

Only include warranty language that your company has approved. Do not invent warranty coverage, part coverage, labor coverage, or time limits.

Can I use the same wording for email?

Yes, but email can usually be a little longer. Text messages should stay brief and easy to read.

Can AI help write HVAC repair follow-up texts?

AI can help clean up rough technician notes into a customer-ready text message, but the final message should be reviewed by a person before it is sent.
