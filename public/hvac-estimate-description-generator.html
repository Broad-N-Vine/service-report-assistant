What to include

Good estimate descriptions work best when the notes explain the issue, the recommended work, why the work is being recommended, and what the customer should understand before approving the estimate.

Do not let the tool create prices, warranty terms, final scope, discounts, financing language, or guaranteed outcomes. Those details should be verified by the office before sending. 

Customer reported AC running but not cooling well. Outdoor coil dirty and filter clogged. Recommended coil cleaning and filter replacement. Customer asked for estimate before approving additional work.
CRM and Approval Review

What should the office check before sending an estimate?

Use this estimate review before sending a quote, saving a CRM note, creating a follow-up task, or handing the estimate to a service manager for approval.

  • Customer clarity: does the wording explain the recommended work in plain English?
  • Confirmed scope: does the estimate only describe work that is actually being proposed?
  • Pricing review: are parts, labor, taxes, discounts, fees, and totals confirmed outside this tool?
  • Approval path: does the customer know how to approve, ask questions, or schedule the work?
  • Declined work: should declined work be saved as a CRM note or future reminder?
  • Recommended work: should the estimate also trigger a customer follow-up or maintenance conversation?
  • Risk language: does the wording avoid unsupported safety, warranty, code, financing, or performance claims?

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is this HVAC Estimate Description Generator for?

It is for HVAC/R owners, office managers, technicians, dispatchers, and admin staff who need to turn rough recommended work notes into clearer estimate, quote, proposal, or customer approval wording.

What kind of notes should I paste into the tool?

Paste the job type, technician findings, recommended work, customer concerns, declined work, customer questions, and any verified office-approved estimate details.

Can I send the generated estimate description directly to a customer?

Use the generated wording as a starting point. Review it before sending and confirm final scope, pricing, parts, labor, taxes, discounts, warranty language, expiration dates, scheduling steps, and customer approval requirements.

Does this tool create estimate pricing?

No. This tool does not create prices, labor rates, part costs, taxes, discounts, balances, financing terms, deposits, or final totals. Any financial details should be verified by your company before use.

Can this tool create warranty, safety, code, or financing language?

No. Do not add warranty, safety, code-compliance, financing, or performance claims unless they are verified and supported by the technician notes and your company policy.

Can this tool define the final project scope?

No. This tool helps clean up wording from the notes provided. Final scope should be reviewed by your company before sending an estimate or quote to a customer.

Can I use the result in Jobber, QuickBooks, or another system?

Yes, you can use the result as a starting point for estimates, quotes, job notes, CRM notes, customer records, or follow-up tasks. Review the wording before adding it to any business system.

What should happen after the estimate is sent?

Save the estimate status in the customer record, schedule a follow-up task if needed, note declined work, and create invoice wording only after the customer approves the work and the work is completed.
