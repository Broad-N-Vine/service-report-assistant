What to include

Good estimate descriptions work best when the notes explain the issue, the recommended work, and what the customer should understand before approving the estimate.

Do not let the tool create prices, warranty terms, final scope, or guaranteed outcomes. Those details should be verified by the office before sending.

Frequently asked questions

Who is this HVAC Estimate Description Generator for?

It is for HVAC/R owners, office managers, technicians, and admin staff who need to turn rough recommended work notes into clearer estimate, quote, or proposal wording.

What kind of notes should I paste into the tool?

Paste the job type, technician findings, recommended work, customer concerns, declined work, and any verified office-approved estimate details.

Can I send the generated estimate description directly to a customer?

Use the generated wording as a starting point. Review it before sending and confirm final scope, pricing, parts, labor, taxes, discounts, warranty language, and customer approval.

Does this tool create estimate pricing?

No. This tool does not create prices, labor rates, part costs, taxes, discounts, balances, or final totals. Any financial details should be verified by your company before use.

Can this tool create warranty or safety language?

No. Do not add warranty, safety, code-compliance, or performance claims unless they are verified and supported by the technician notes and your company policy.

Can this tool define the final project scope?

No. This tool helps clean up wording from the notes provided. Final scope should be reviewed by your company before sending an estimate or quote to a customer.

Can I use the result in Jobber, QuickBooks, or another system?

Yes, you can use the result as a starting point for estimates, quotes, job notes, or customer records. Review the wording before adding it to any business system.
