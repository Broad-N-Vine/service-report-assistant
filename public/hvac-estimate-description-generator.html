Frequently asked questions

Who is this HVAC Estimate Description Generator for?

It is for HVAC/R owners, office managers, technicians, and admin staff who need cleaner wording for customer estimates, quotes, and repair recommendations.

What information should I include?

Include the job type, recommended work, technician findings, customer concern, equipment notes, parts or repairs being quoted, and any important follow-up items.

Does this tool create pricing?

No. This tool does not create prices, labor rates, part costs, taxes, discounts, deposits, payment terms, or final totals. Pricing should always be entered and verified by your company.

Can I send the generated estimate wording directly to a customer?

Use it as a starting point. Review the scope, pricing, parts, labor, warranty language, exclusions, scheduling details, and safety-related wording before sending the estimate.

Can this tool make warranty, code, or safety claims?

No. Do not include warranty, code-compliance, safety, or performance claims unless they are verified and supported by the technician notes and your company policy.

Can I use the result in Jobber, QuickBooks, or another quoting system?

Yes, you can use the result as a starting point for estimate descriptions, quote notes, customer records, or internal job notes. Review the wording before adding it to any system.

Does this replace estimating or field service software?

No. This tool only helps clean up written descriptions. It does not replace quoting, estimating, job costing, scheduling, invoicing, payments, bookkeeping, or field service software.
