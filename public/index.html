Customer Workflow

Built around what happens after the service call.

HVAC/R paperwork is not just documentation. It affects billing, customer trust, follow-up timing, future estimates, maintenance opportunities, and what gets saved in the customer record.

For the customer

Clear wording helps customers understand what was found, what was completed, what was recommended, and what happens next.

For the office

Review notes help the office check pricing, parts, warranty language, safety wording, declined work, and follow-up tasks.

For the business

Cleaner paperwork makes it easier to copy text into Jobber, QuickBooks, customer records, invoices, estimates, and internal notes.

Review First

AI-generated paperwork should always be reviewed before customer use.

HVAC AI Helper is designed to help create cleaner drafts from rough notes. It does not replace technician judgment, office review, pricing approval, warranty policy, safety review, food-safety review, or your company’s customer communication standards.

Review before using: Confirm parts, pricing, labor, measurements, temperatures, refrigerant details, warranty language, customer-specific details, safety wording, food-safety wording, final operating condition, and recommendations before sending anything to a customer or saving it in a business system.
