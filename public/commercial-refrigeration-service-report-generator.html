Frequently asked questions

Who is this Commercial Refrigeration Service Report Generator for?

It is for HVAC/R owners, office managers, technicians, and admin staff who need to turn rough refrigeration service notes into cleaner service reports, invoice wording, customer messages, and internal summaries.

What kind of notes should I paste into the tool?

Paste the job type, equipment type, customer complaint, technician findings, completed work, verified readings, parts used, recommendations, declined work, and any office review notes.

Can this tool create temperature readings or pressure readings?

No. The tool should not create temperatures, pressures, refrigerant amounts, model numbers, serial numbers, or final operating conditions. Those details must come from the technician or office records.

Can this tool make food safety claims?

No. Do not use generated wording to claim product is safe, food is safe, or temperature compliance has been confirmed unless those details are verified by your company and supported by the service record.

Can I send the generated report directly to a customer?

Use the generated wording as a starting point. Review it before sending and confirm temperatures, pressures, refrigerant details, parts, pricing, warranty language, food safety wording, and final operating conditions.

Can I use the result in Jobber, QuickBooks, or another system?

Yes, you can use the result as a starting point for service reports, invoice descriptions, customer notes, or internal job summaries. Review the wording before adding it to any business system.

Does this replace a refrigeration technician’s diagnosis?

No. This tool only helps clean up wording from technician notes. It does not diagnose equipment, verify readings, confirm food safety, determine compliance, or replace technician judgment.

What should I review before sending refrigeration paperwork?

Review temperatures, pressures, refrigerant details, model numbers, parts, pricing, warranty language, food safety wording, customer approval, final operating conditions, and company rules before sending.
