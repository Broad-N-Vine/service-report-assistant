Reminder

Review everything before sending it to a customer

HVAC AI Helper is designed to help clean up wording from technician notes. It does not replace a technician, office manager, service manager, accounting system, field service platform, or company review process.

Review before sending: Confirm service details, parts, pricing, measurements, readings, warranty language, safety-related wording, customer-specific notes, and final recommendations before using any AI-generated or template-based text.
