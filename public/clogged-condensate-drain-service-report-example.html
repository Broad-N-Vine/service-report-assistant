Quick Answer

How should you write a clogged condensate drain service report?

A clogged condensate drain service report should explain the customer’s reported issue, what the technician found, what was done to clear or inspect the drain, and what follow-up items should be reviewed with the customer.

The report should stay factual. Do not invent drain condition, water damage, safety claims, warranty terms, or final system condition unless those details are confirmed in the technician notes.

How to use it

Write a better drain service report in three steps

1. Start with the complaint

Note whether the customer reported water near the unit, a clogged drain, a float switch issue, system shutdown, or other drainage concern.

2. Describe confirmed work

Include only the work actually completed, such as clearing the line, flushing the drain, checking drainage, or inspecting the pan.

3. Review before sending

Confirm water damage notes, drain condition, pricing, recommendations, warranty language, and customer-specific details before sending.

Technician Notes

Example rough technician notes

Customer reported water near indoor unit. Found condensate drain line clogged. Cleared drain line and flushed with water. Checked drain pan and confirmed water draining after service. Recommended routine maintenance and monitoring for future clogs.

These notes are not perfect, but they include the customer complaint, technician finding, completed work, post-service check, and recommendation.

Example Report

Clogged condensate drain service report example

HVAC Service Report

Customer / Job:
Residential AC service call

Service Date:
[Date]

Reason for Visit:
Customer reported water near the indoor unit.

Equipment / System:
Residential AC system.

Technician Findings:
Technician found the condensate drain line clogged.

Work Completed:
Cleared the condensate drain line and flushed it with water. Checked the drain pan and confirmed water was draining after service.

Parts / Materials:
No parts documented in the technician notes.

System Notes:
Drainage was checked after the line was cleared. Final system operation and any additional readings should be confirmed before sending.

Recommendations:
Customer should monitor the drain area and schedule routine maintenance to help reduce the chance of future drain clogs.

Customer Notes:
No customer-specific notes documented.

Review Notes:
Confirm pricing, final system condition, water damage notes, warranty language, and any recommendations before sending to the customer.
Review before sending: Confirm drain condition, water damage notes, completed work, pricing, warranty language, customer-specific notes, and final recommendations before using this report.
Before and After

Vague vs. better drain service wording

Too vague

Fixed drain.

This does not explain what was wrong, what work was completed, or what the customer should know.

Better

Found clogged condensate drain line, cleared and flushed drain, checked drain pan, and confirmed drainage after service.

This gives the customer and office staff a clearer record of the work completed.

Invoice Wording

Clogged condensate drain invoice wording example

Cleared clogged condensate drain line, flushed drain, checked drain pan, and confirmed drainage after service.

Invoice wording should usually be shorter than the full service report. Confirm pricing, parts, labor, taxes, and warranty language before sending any invoice.

Common Mistakes

Common clogged drain report mistakes to avoid

  • Being too vague: avoid notes like “fixed leak” without explaining the drain issue.
  • Inventing water damage: only mention damage if it was actually documented.
  • Overpromising: do not say the drain will not clog again.
  • Skipping the recommendation: include maintenance or monitoring recommendations when supported by the notes.
  • Forgetting review: pricing, warranty wording, final condition, and customer notes should be checked before sending.

FAQ

FAQ

How do you write a service report for a clogged condensate drain?

Explain the customer complaint, what the technician found, what was done to clear or inspect the drain, and what the customer should monitor or review after the visit.

Should the report mention water damage?

Only mention water damage if it was documented in the technician notes. Do not invent damage, severity, cause, or repair responsibility.

Should the report say the drain will not clog again?

No. Avoid guarantees unless your company has specifically approved that wording. A safer report can recommend maintenance or monitoring.

Can I use this for an invoice description?

Yes, but invoice wording should usually be shorter than the full report. Focus on the confirmed work performed, such as clearing and flushing the drain line.

Can AI help write clogged drain service reports?

AI can help clean up rough technician notes, but the final report should be reviewed by a person before it is sent to a customer.
