Overview

HVAC AI Helper provides free HVAC/R paperwork tools, templates, and guides. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and how visitors can contact us with privacy-related questions.

This site is intended to help with service report wording, invoice descriptions, follow-up messages, estimate descriptions, technician note examples, and related paperwork. All generated or template-based wording should be reviewed before being sent to a customer.

Information you choose to provide

You may choose to provide information when using the site, including:

  • Your email address when signing up for templates or helpful tips
  • Information you send through email or contact links
  • Technician notes or job details that you choose to paste into a tool

Do not enter sensitive personal information, private customer information, payment details, passwords, confidential business information, emergency service requests, or anything you do not want processed by website tools or third-party services.

Email signup and marketing

If you enter your email address, we may use it to send the requested template, tool result, HVAC/R paperwork examples, helpful tips, and related updates.

You can unsubscribe from future emails by using the unsubscribe link provided in the email.

We do not sell your email address.

Tool inputs and generated wording

The tools on this site are designed to help turn rough HVAC/R notes into cleaner paperwork examples. At this stage, the tools and templates should not be treated as final professional, legal, safety, warranty, accounting, tax, or compliance advice.

Always review generated or template-based wording before using it. Confirm parts, pricing, measurements, temperatures, warranty language, safety-related statements, code-related statements, customer details, and final recommendations.
