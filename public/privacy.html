Privacy Policy

This page explains how HVAC AI Helper handles information submitted through the site, including free AI generator requests, email signup forms, contact emails, and affiliate links.

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Overview

What this policy covers

HVAC AI Helper provides free HVAC/R paperwork tools, examples, templates, and guides. The site is intended to help users create cleaner drafts from rough technician notes, including service reports, invoice descriptions, customer follow-ups, estimate wording, maintenance messages, commercial refrigeration reports, and related office review notes.

This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the site, how that information may be used, and what you should avoid submitting.

Important: Do not submit private customer details, payment information, passwords, confidential business records, protected personal information, or sensitive service records through the generators, signup forms, or email.
Information Collected

Information you may provide

Depending on how you use the site, you may provide some or all of the following:

  • Email address: used to complete free generator requests or to sign up for email updates.
  • Company name: optional context you may enter into a generator form.
  • Customer first name: optional context you may enter to help shape a customer-facing draft.
  • Job type: such as repair, maintenance, estimate, service report, or refrigeration call.
  • Technician notes: rough notes you paste into a generator to create a draft.
  • Verified details: optional readings, review notes, equipment details, or office notes you choose to provide.
  • Contact email content: information you send directly by email when contacting the site.

The site is designed for general paperwork support. It is not intended to receive sensitive customer data, medical information, financial account details, passwords, protected personal information, or confidential records.

AI Generator Requests

How generator information is used

When you use a free AI generator, the information you submit may be used to create the requested draft. This may include service report wording, invoice descriptions, customer follow-up messages, estimate wording, maintenance pitch wording, commercial refrigeration reports, internal summaries, or review notes.

Generator outputs are created as drafts. You are responsible for reviewing the result before using it with customers, invoices, CRM records, business systems, service records, estimates, or internal workflows.

Do submit

General job notes, non-sensitive equipment information, completed work, recommendations, and verified details that are safe to use in a draft.

Do not submit

Payment details, passwords, full customer profiles, private addresses, confidential business records, or sensitive personal information.

Always review

Confirm parts, pricing, labor, measurements, warranty language, safety wording, food-safety wording, and company policy before using AI-generated text.

Email Signup

Email forms and MailerLite

Some pages include an email signup form for receiving HVAC/R paperwork examples, templates, tips, and occasional helpful emails. These signup forms may be managed through MailerLite.

If you sign up through an email form, your email address may be used to send the requested materials and related HVAC/R paperwork updates. You can unsubscribe from future emails using the unsubscribe link included in email messages.

Submitting an email address inside a generator form is separate from signing up for the email sequence. To receive the email sequence, use the signup form shown on the site.

Contact Email

Messages sent by email

If you contact HVAC AI Helper by email, the information you provide may be used to respond to your message, review feedback, troubleshoot a page issue, improve the site, or understand what tools and examples may be useful.

Please avoid sending sensitive customer information, payment details, passwords, confidential business records, or private service records by email.

Data Safety

Your responsibility when using the tools

You are responsible for deciding what information is safe to submit and whether generated wording is appropriate for your business, customer, invoice, service record, CRM, estimate, or internal workflow.

Review-first reminder: AI-generated wording should not be treated as final. Review the result for accuracy, tone, pricing, warranty language, safety wording, food-safety wording, customer-specific details, company policy, and compliance with your business practices before using it.
