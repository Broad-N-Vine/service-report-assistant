Overview

HVAC AI Helper provides free HVAC/R paperwork tools, templates, examples, and guides. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and how visitors can contact us with privacy-related questions.

This site is intended to help with service report wording, invoice descriptions, follow-up messages, estimate descriptions, maintenance plan pitches, technician note examples, commercial refrigeration reports, and related HVAC/R paperwork.

All generated or template-based wording should be reviewed before being sent to a customer, added to an invoice, copied into a business system, or stored in a service record.

Information you choose to provide

You may choose to provide information when using the site, including:

  • Your email address when signing up for templates, tool updates, or helpful tips
  • Information you send through email or contact links
  • Technician notes or job details that you choose to paste into a tool
  • Feedback, corrections, or suggestions that you choose to send

Do not enter sensitive personal information, private customer information, payment details, passwords, login credentials, confidential business information, emergency service requests, or anything you do not want processed by website tools or third-party services.

Email signup and marketing

If you enter your email address, we may use it to send the requested template, tool result, HVAC/R paperwork examples, helpful tips, and related updates.

Email signup forms may be handled by a third-party email service provider. That provider may process your information according to its own privacy policy, terms, and settings.

You can unsubscribe from future emails by using the unsubscribe link provided in the email.

We do not sell your email address.

Tool inputs and generated wording

The tools on this site are designed to help turn rough HVAC/R notes into cleaner paperwork drafts. You may choose to paste job details, technician notes, findings, completed work, recommendations, or related service information into a tool.

At this stage, the tools and templates should not be treated as final professional, legal, safety, warranty, accounting, tax, food safety, code compliance, or regulatory advice.

Always review generated or template-based wording before using it. Confirm parts, pricing, measurements, temperatures, pressures, refrigerant details, warranty language, safety-related statements, food safety wording, code-related statements, customer details, and final recommendations.
