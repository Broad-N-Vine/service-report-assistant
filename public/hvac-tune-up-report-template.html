HVAC Tune-Up Report Template

Use this template to document HVAC maintenance visits more clearly.

This page gives HVAC/R owners, office staff, and technicians a simple structure for turning tune-up notes into a cleaner customer-ready report.

Quick answer

A good HVAC tune-up report should summarize the service visit, list the basic checks performed, document what the technician found, explain any completed work, and clearly note recommended next steps.

The goal is not to overcomplicate the paperwork. The goal is to make the tune-up easy for the customer, office staff, and technician to understand later.

What to include in an HVAC tune-up report

  • Customer name or job reference
  • Service date
  • Equipment type
  • Reason for visit or tune-up type
  • Basic system checks performed
  • Filter condition
  • Coil condition
  • Thermostat or control notes
  • Drain line or condensate notes, when applicable
  • Temperature split or operating notes, when applicable
  • Work completed during the visit
  • Recommended follow-up items
  • Customer declined items, if applicable
  • Missing details that should be reviewed before sending

Simple HVAC tune-up report template

Copy this structure and adjust it to match the technician’s actual notes. 

HVAC Tune-Up Report

Customer / Job:
[Customer name or job reference]

Service Date:
[Date]

Equipment / System:
[AC system, furnace, heat pump, rooftop unit, etc.]

Reason for Visit:
[Maintenance tune-up, seasonal check, follow-up visit, etc.]

Work Performed:
- Checked thermostat operation
- Checked filter condition
- Inspected accessible electrical components
- Inspected accessible indoor and outdoor components
- Checked general system operation
- Documented technician findings

Technician Findings:
[Summarize what the technician found in plain English.]

Completed Work:
[Summarize work completed during the tune-up.]

Recommendations:
[List any recommended cleaning, repairs, filter replacement, maintenance plan follow-up, or further evaluation.]

Customer Notes:
[Include declined work, requested follow-up, or special notes if applicable.]

Review Notes:
Review all details before sending to the customer. Confirm measurements, parts, pricing, warranty language, safety claims, and final recommendations.

Example tune-up report

Here is a simple example based on rough technician notes. 

HVAC Tune-Up Report

Customer / Job:
Residential AC maintenance visit

Service Date:
[Add date]

Equipment / System:
Central air conditioning system

Reason for Visit:
Seasonal AC maintenance tune-up

Work Performed:
The technician completed a maintenance visit and checked general system operation. The filter condition was reviewed, the outdoor coil was inspected, and system performance was documented during the visit.

Technician Findings:
The filter was clogged and the outdoor coil was dirty. The system was cooling during the visit with an 18-degree temperature split.

Completed Work:
The technician checked system operation and documented the condition of the filter and outdoor coil.

Recommendations:
Replace the clogged filter and consider outdoor coil cleaning. Routine maintenance may help keep service reminders organized and make future system checks easier to track.

Customer Notes:
Customer declined coil cleaning today.

Review Notes:
Review this report before sending. Confirm all service details, recommendations, pricing, warranty language, and safety-related wording.
