What to include

Good follow-up messages need enough detail to explain what happened without overpromising. Include what was found, what was completed, and what the customer should do next.

Avoid making warranty, safety, or performance promises unless those details are verified. 

Replaced weak capacitor. System cooling with 18-degree temperature split. Filter clogged. Recommended filter replacement and coil cleaning. Customer declined coil cleaning today.

Frequently asked questions

Who is this HVAC Customer Follow-Up Text Generator for?

It is for HVAC/R owners, office managers, technicians, and admin staff who need to send clearer customer follow-up texts or emails after service visits.

What kind of notes should I paste into the tool?

Paste the job type, technician findings, completed work, customer concerns, recommended next steps, declined work, and any follow-up items that should be mentioned to the customer.

Can I send the generated message directly to a customer?

Use the generated message as a starting point. Review it before sending and confirm that all service details, recommendations, pricing, warranty language, and safety-related wording are accurate.

Should this be used as a text message or an email?

Use a text message for short updates and simple follow-ups. Use an email when the customer needs more detail, a longer explanation, or written documentation after the service visit.

Can this tool make promises about repairs, warranties, or system safety?

No. Do not promise that a system is fully fixed, safe, under warranty, or guaranteed to perform unless those details are verified and clearly supported by the technician notes.

Can I use the result in Jobber, QuickBooks, or another system?

Yes, you can use the result as a starting point for customer messages, job notes, or follow-up records. Review the wording before adding it to any business system.

Does this replace customer service or job-management software?

No. This tool helps draft customer communication. It does not replace scheduling, dispatching, invoicing, payments, CRM, or customer-management software.
