Recommended workflow

1. Start with rough notes

Gather what the customer reported, what the technician found, what was completed, what was recommended, and what still needs office review.

2. Choose the right AI tool

Use the service report generator for the most complete output, or choose a specific tool for invoice wording, follow-ups, estimates, maintenance pitches, or refrigeration reports.

3. Review before using

Confirm service details, parts, pricing, readings, temperatures, warranty language, safety wording, food safety wording, customer approval, and company-specific rules before use.
