Review First

Important reminder before using any AI-generated HVAC/R wording

AI-generated wording should always be reviewed before it is sent to a customer, added to an invoice, used in an estimate, or saved in a customer record.

Do not let AI invent: prices, parts, labor, taxes, discounts, warranty terms, safety claims, code-compliance claims, refrigerant amounts, temperature readings, customer approvals, final repair status, or legal language.

Use these tools to speed up drafting and cleanup, not to replace technician judgment, office review, company policy, customer approval, or final billing review.
