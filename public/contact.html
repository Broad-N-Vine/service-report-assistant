What to include in your message

For tool feedback: Include the page name, what you expected to happen, and what looked confusing or incorrect.
For correction requests: Include the page URL and the wording you think should be reviewed.
For affiliate questions: Include the resource name and the page where you saw the recommendation.
For privacy questions: Include your question and the email address involved, if relevant.
For partnership ideas: Include the company, product, or resource name and explain how it helps HVAC/R paperwork, scheduling, invoicing, bookkeeping, or customer follow-up.

Before using generated wording

The tools and templates on this site are meant to help create clearer drafts. They do not replace professional review, company policy, technician judgment, accounting software, legal advice, warranty review, code review, or safety review.

AI-generated wording is a draft. Always review service details, parts, pricing, measurements, warranty language, safety-related wording, customer-specific details, estimate scope, customer approval requirements, and final recommendations before sending anything to a customer.
