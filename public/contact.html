Privacy and Safety

Before sending information

HVAC AI Helper is intended for general paperwork support. Do not submit private customer details, sensitive personal information, payment information, account credentials, or confidential business records.

Review-first reminder: AI-generated wording should always be checked by a person before customer use. Confirm service details, parts, prices, labor, measurements, warranty language, safety wording, food-safety wording, customer-specific details, and company policy before using generated text.
