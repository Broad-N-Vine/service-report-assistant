For partnerships

Do not send sensitive information

Please do not send private customer information, payment details, passwords, login credentials, confidential business information, emergency requests, or anything that should not be reviewed through email.

If you are using an AI tool on this site, avoid pasting personal customer data or confidential business details into the tool. Keep notes practical and limited to the paperwork wording you need help drafting.

AI output review

HVAC AI Helper tools create draft wording. Generated text should be reviewed before it is sent to a customer, added to an invoice, used in a service record, or copied into any business system.

Confirm service details, parts, pricing, measurements, temperatures, warranty language, safety wording, food safety wording, customer-specific details, company rules, and final recommendations before using any generated result.
