Soft text message

Thanks again for having us out today. Based on the visit, routine maintenance may be a helpful way to keep the system checked regularly and make it easier to catch common issues early. Let us know if you would like details about available maintenance options.

Thank you for choosing us for your HVAC/R service visit. During the visit, our technician noted items that may benefit from routine maintenance. A maintenance plan can help keep service reminders organized and give your system regular attention. Please contact us if you would like to review the options.

Frequently asked questions

Who is this HVAC Maintenance Plan Pitch Generator for?

It is for HVAC/R owners, office managers, technicians, and admin staff who need simple wording to explain maintenance options after a service visit.

What kind of notes should I paste into the tool?

Paste the job type, technician findings, maintenance concerns, customer situation, declined work, recommended follow-up items, and any notes that support why maintenance may be worth discussing.

Should this tool make a hard sales pitch?

No. The goal is to explain maintenance options clearly and professionally without sounding pushy. The wording should help the customer understand the option, not pressure them.

Can this tool promise lower bills or prevent breakdowns?

No. Avoid guaranteed savings, lower utility bill claims, breakdown prevention claims, warranty promises, safety claims, or long-term performance promises unless those details are verified by your company.

Can I send the generated maintenance pitch directly to a customer?

Use it as a starting point. Review the wording first and confirm that all maintenance plan details, pricing, included services, exclusions, and company policies are accurate.

Can I use the result in Jobber, QuickBooks, or another system?

Yes, you can use the result as a starting point for customer follow-up notes, maintenance reminders, estimate notes, or office records. Review it before saving or sending.

Does this replace a written maintenance agreement?

No. This tool helps draft simple explanation wording. It does not replace your company’s official maintenance agreement, contract terms, service policies, pricing, or legal review.
