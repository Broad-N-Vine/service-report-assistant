Frequently asked questions

Who is this HVAC Maintenance Plan Pitch Generator for?

It is for HVAC/R owners, office managers, technicians, and admin staff who need a clearer way to follow up about maintenance options after a service visit.

What kind of notes should I paste into the tool?

Paste the job type, technician findings, completed work, system condition notes, customer concerns, and any maintenance recommendation supported by the visit.

Can I send the generated pitch directly to a customer?

Use the generated pitch as a starting point. Review it before sending and confirm plan details, pricing, terms, benefits, warranty language, and company-specific maintenance agreement rules.

Does this tool create maintenance plan pricing?

No. This tool does not create prices, discounts, contract terms, membership benefits, taxes, or final totals. Any financial or agreement details should be verified by your company before use.

Can this tool promise fewer repairs or lower utility bills?

No. Avoid promising fewer repairs, lower utility bills, longer equipment life, safety, or guaranteed performance unless those claims are verified and supported by your company policy.

Can I use the result in Jobber, QuickBooks, or another system?

Yes, you can use the result as a starting point for customer messages, job notes, follow-up records, or maintenance agreement conversations. Review the wording before adding it to any business system.

Does this replace a maintenance agreement?

No. This tool helps draft customer communication. It does not replace your actual maintenance agreement, contract terms, pricing sheet, warranty policy, or customer approval process.

What should I review before sending a maintenance pitch?

Review plan details, pricing, terms, benefits, warranty language, customer approval, company agreement rules, customer-specific details, and any performance or savings claims before sending.
