FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is this tool for?

This tool is for HVAC/R owners, office managers, technicians, and admin staff who need help turning rough service notes into clearer paperwork.

Can I use the generated text without reviewing it?

No. Use generated text as a starting point only. Review service details, pricing, parts, warranty language, safety-related wording, and customer-specific notes before sending.

Does this replace field service, invoicing, or accounting software?

No. This tool helps clean up wording. It does not replace job management, scheduling, invoicing, payments, bookkeeping, accounting, or tax software.
